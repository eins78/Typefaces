About

Thanks to this twitter thread by @gontijodesign (backup), the Velvetyne Type Foundry and all the type designers releasing their work under permissive licenses.

The fonts are git submodules of eins78/Typefaces, one per family. Each family keeps its own licence, shown in the playground and shipped next to the font files.
